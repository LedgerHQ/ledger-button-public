Support XRP testnet: XRP networks are read from the dApp config, where a network `id` is the XRPL `network_id` (`"0"` mainnet, `"1"` testnet), used as the backend chain id and the network reported to dApps. Test network currencies with no icon of their own (e.g. `ripple_testnet`) show their mainnet currency icon.
