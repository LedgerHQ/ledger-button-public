Track the device connection funnel: `device_connection_selected` when the user starts a connection, then `device_connection_succeeded` or `device_connection_failed` (with `error_type` and `error_category`). All three carry `connection_type` (`bluetooth` | `usb`) and `ledger_sync_user_id` when Ledger Sync is active
