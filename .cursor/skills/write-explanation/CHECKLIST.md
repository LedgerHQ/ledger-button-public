# Quality Checklist (Explanation)

Mark **N/A** only where the criterion explicitly allows it.

- [ ] **Minimum content threshold**: Substantial content (~200+ words, or equivalent in images/diagrams, code, or combination). *Must have.*
- [ ] **Clear and concise language**: Clear, simple language; avoid unnecessary jargon.
- [ ] **Completeness**: Covers the topic without gaps for the stated scope.
- [ ] **Publication readiness**: No TBD, TBW, WIP, or placeholders.
- [ ] **Title and structure coherence**: Title matches content; sections follow a logical flow.
- [ ] **Conceptual clarity**: Concepts explained with appropriate depth and examples.
- [ ] **Logical flow**: Information builds progressively; relationships between ideas are clear.
- [ ] **Contextual information**: Explains why and when things matter, not only what they are.
- [ ] **Introduction clarity**: Purpose and scope clear; links to prerequisites if needed.
- [ ] **Visual elements effectiveness**: Any diagrams or visuals are referenced and contextualised.
- [ ] **Cross-document navigation**: Links to related explanation, reference, how-to, tutorial.
- [ ] **Main takeaways**: Summary of key points (nice to have).
- [ ] **Image accessibility**: Images have meaningful alt text; descriptive length. *If no images, mark N/A.*
- [ ] **Warning and note implementation**: If risks or caveats exist, 2–3 per major section max; clear, action-oriented; placed just before relevant content. *If not needed, mark N/A.*
