# American English style guide: source reference

This skill aligns with the **Chicago Manual of Style** (18th edition, American English), published by the University of Chicago Press.

## Official source

- **Title:** The Chicago Manual of Style, 18th edition
- **Publisher:** University of Chicago Press, 2024.
- **URL:** [chicagomanualofstyle.org](https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/book/ed18/frontmatter/toc.html)
- **DOI:** <https://doi.org/10.7208/cmos18>
- **Audience:** Authors, editors, and anyone producing written content in American English.

## Structure of the Chicago Manual of Style (18th edition)

The guide is organised as follows:

1. **Part I: Publishing and Editing** — Manuscript preparation, illustrations, rights and permissions.
2. **Part II: Style and Usage** — Grammar and usage, punctuation, spelling, names and terms, numbers, abbreviations, quotations.
3. **Part III: Source Citations and Indexes** — Citation formats, examples, and indexing.

The **18th edition (2024)** includes updated guidance on grammar and usage by Bryan A. Garner, and is the current authoritative reference for American English style.

## How this skill uses the guide

Spelling, punctuation, vocabulary, and examples are summarised in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and [VOCABULARY.md](VOCABULARY.md); [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md) gives phrasing examples. For exact wording and the full house-style list, refer to the official guide.
